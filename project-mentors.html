About this project

The project

Find Your Mentor is the live result of a Figma assignment — a one-page mentor marketplace. It opens with the promise that “1,600+ mentors are just a free trial session away”, then gets straight to work: a search field that filters by company or skills, and an “Explore all mentors” grid of profiles.

Every mentor card carries the same useful details — photo, a verified tick, role and company, years of experience, star rating with review count, and a “Starting from $X.00 / Month” price with a “Book a Free Trial” button. New arrivals wear a small “New Mentor” badge, and a little JavaScript powers the search so the cards filter as you type.
