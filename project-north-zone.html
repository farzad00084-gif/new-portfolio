About this project

The project

NORTH ZONE is a “Kabul → the world” visa agency site. The hero features a signature SVG route map with lines radiating from Kabul to Tehran, Dushanbe, Tashkent, Dubai, Berlin and Rome — each with its own embassy timeline — followed by a passport-style stats strip, visa services grouped by region, a four-checkpoint process, client testimonials and a working contact layout.

The design brief was no JavaScript, so every effect — the numbered stamps, the destination cards, the route map — is pure HTML and CSS. The site lists its real details too: an office in Shar-e-Naw, Kabul, phone +93 729 375 643, info@northzone.af, and hours Saturday–Thursday 8:00–16:00.

How it works

Four checkpoints to your visa

Consultation

Destination, purpose of travel and existing documents are reviewed to confirm which visa fits.

Document preparation

Forms, translations, bank statements and invitation letters — checked twice against embassy rules.

Embassy submission

Appointment booked and the file submitted in person, so nothing is lost or delayed.

Visa ready

The decision is tracked, and the client is called the moment the passport can be collected in Kabul.
