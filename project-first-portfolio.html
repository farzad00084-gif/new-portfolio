About this project

The project

My First Portfolio — titled “Ahmad Farzad — Portfolio” — is the website I built while I was still learning front-end development. It opens from Kabul, Afghanistan with the tagline “organized, adaptable, and ready to contribute” and walks visitors through who I am, what I've studied, and how to reach me.

The site is split into three simple pages. The homepage introduces me and links to the rest; Skills & Education covers my Ghazi High School diploma, certificates, language proficiency in English, Dari, Pashto and Turkish, and the interpersonal and technical skills I bring to work — including the “Currently at Upskill” front-end training block that started all of this. Contact shares my email, phone and WhatsApp with a single “Send an Email” button.
